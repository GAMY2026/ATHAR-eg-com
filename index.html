OUR STORY

Born From Memory.

ATHAR means “trace” in Arabic — the quiet mark a person leaves behind. We create fragrances that do exactly that: blending rich oud, soft florals and warm amber into scents that stay with you long after you’ve left the room.

«أثر» هو ما يبقى بعد رحيلك. من هنا بدأت حكايتنا: عطور نختار مكوّناتها بعناية، ونمزجها بحب، لتمنحك بصمة خاصة لا تشبه أحدًا.

01 — CRAFT

Carefully Composed

Every blend is balanced note by note, from the first spritz to the last trace.

02 — LONGEVITY

Made To Last

Concentrated Eau de Parfum that stays with you from morning to night.

03 — SIGNATURE

Truly Yours

Scents designed to feel personal, memorable and impossible to mistake.
